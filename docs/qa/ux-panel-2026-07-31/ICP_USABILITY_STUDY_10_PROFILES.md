## Path to higher ICP scores (not inventing 5.0)

1. Ship **ICP-P0-1/2** (MSSP enter-client + open action).  
2. Ship **ICP-P1-1…7** (mute UI, dual-pane findings, Home triage-first, Executive on Operate, board honesty strip, continuous health on Shift).  
3. Ship **ICP-P1-8…10, 15–16** (automation honesty + path CTA cleanup).

### To purchase ≥3.5 for CISO/board (P03/P10)

1. ≥1 NDA design-partner reference (real, not invented).  
2. Routine **Fully Measured multi-hop** demo path.  
3. Trust pack fill: pen-test summary path, commercial residuals labeled with dates.  
4. SCIM or contractual provisioning SLA for enterprise order form.  
5. Keep refuse list — do not greenwash to inflate purchase score.

- Fake customer logos / ARR / SOC 2 Type II claim  
- Live Atomic/Caldera/SharpHound  
- Certification theater in compliance packs
